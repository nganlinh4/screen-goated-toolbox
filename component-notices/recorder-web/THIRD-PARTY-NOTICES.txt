SGT Screen Recorder web interface third-party notices

The recorder web interface includes React, Radix UI, Motion, and other
production packages resolved from screen-record/package-lock.json. Their
copyright and license terms remain with their respective authors.

THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.json is a deterministic inventory of the resolved
production packages plus the complete bytes, names, and SHA-256 digests of
their packaged license, notice, copying, and copyright files. When a package
declares a standard license without packaging its own copy, the inventory maps
it to one complete deduplicated license text from the same resolved dependency
graph.
