SGT Screen Recorder worker third-party notices

The worker is built from the Screen Goated Toolbox source tree and includes
open-source Rust dependencies under their respective licenses. The
package also contains THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.json: a deterministic inventory of
every resolved x64 Rust package plus the complete bytes, names, and SHA-256
digests of every license, notice, copying, and copyright file distributed by
those packages. When a crate declares a standard license without packaging its
own copy, the inventory maps that crate to one complete deduplicated license
text from the same resolved dependency graph.

Major native and media dependencies include:

- windows-rs (MIT OR Apache-2.0)
- windows-capture (MIT)
- wry and tao (Apache-2.0 OR MIT)
- wgpu (MIT OR Apache-2.0)
- Symphonia (MPL-2.0)
- resvg and usvg (MIT OR Apache-2.0), tiny-skia (BSD-3-Clause)
- ISO 639 catalog data derived from isolang 2.4.0 (Apache-2.0)
- ringbuf, cpal, hound, and wasapi (see the packaged license inventory)

This notice does not alter or replace any dependency license.
