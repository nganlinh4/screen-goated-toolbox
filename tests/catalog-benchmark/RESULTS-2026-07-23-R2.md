# Catalog Benchmark Results — 2026-07-23 R2

This decision record supersedes the same-day baseline after ordinary reasoning
was disabled or reduced to each endpoint's lowest supported setting. The raw
report is `target/catalog-benchmark/reasoning-policy-20260723-r2/`.
It predates benchmark protocol v2. Its vision calls streamed and did not use the
same JSON-mode setting as the production OCR preset, so its vision latency is
historical diagnostic evidence—not a comparable catalog run. The decisions
below are the bootstrap catalog snapshot; subsequent performance or priority
changes require production-matching v2 evidence.

- Planned and attempted cells: 450
- Successful responses: 340
- Request errors: 108
- Empty or malformed responses: 2
- Text: 19 models × 10 levels
- Vision: 13 models × 10 coordinate levels + 13 models × 10 OCR levels
- Request pacing: at least 4 seconds between calls to the same provider
- Accuracy: objective coordinate/OCR scoring plus manual review of every
  successful translation against its case rubric

The report records the effective reasoning policy on every attempt. Provider
failures are availability evidence, not zero accuracy. Human accuracy is the
share of explicit rubric criteria satisfied by successful responses. `*`
denotes sparse evidence and cannot qualify a model to lead a general chain.

## Catalog Decisions

- Keep Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite as the image default. Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite has
  a 0.23-second lower representative median, but 3.5 is perfect on both suites
  and has a substantially lower representative P95.
- Make Cerebras GLM 4.7 the text default. With reasoning disabled it is both
  faster and more stable than every other general, durable text endpoint while
  retaining 93% human accuracy.
- Text retry order: Cerebras GLM 4.7, Groq GPT-OSS 120B, Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite,
  Cerebras GPT-OSS 120B, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, Google Gemma 4 26B, Google
  Gemma 4 31B. The first three deliberately span providers; within that
  reliability constraint, latency wins over small intelligence differences.
- Image retry order: Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, Google Gemma
  4 31B, Google Gemma 4 26B, Cerebras Gemma 4 31B, Qwen 3.6. Qwen remains only
  as the last OCR-capable fallback; it must not enter coordinate-grounding
  chains.
- Raise Cerebras GPT-OSS 120B from intelligence tier 4 to 5 because its
  successful-output accuracy now matches the Groq endpoint under the shared
  low-reasoning policy.
- Rename Cerebras GLM 4.7 from “Careful, slow” to “Careful”, and Google Gemma
  4 26B from “Good, very slow” to “Balanced”; the old speed labels are no
  longer true under the production reasoning policy.
- Do not promote low-RPD Gemini models from sparse results. Quota failures are
  part of consistency, even when successful cells are fast and accurate.
